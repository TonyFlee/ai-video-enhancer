1. WHAT THE APP DOES
--------------------
AI Video Enhancer increases the frame rate of a video file (for example 24, 25
or 30 fps up to 60 or 120 fps) by generating brand-new intermediate frames with
a neural network (RIFE). It runs entirely on your own computer.


2. YOUR VIDEOS STAY ON YOUR MACHINE
-----------------------------------
Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no account, no telemetry and no
analytics. The app binds to 127.0.0.1 (localhost) only, so no other computer on
your network can reach it. Files are written to a local temp folder that is
cleared automatically.


3. YOUR ORIGINAL FILE IS SAFE
-----------------------------
The app never edits, overwrites or deletes the video you select. Results are
written to a new file. Keep your own copy of the source video anyway - you are
responsible for your own footage and for anything you publish from it.
